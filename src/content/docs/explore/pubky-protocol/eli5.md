title: "Explaining the Pubky Protocol Like You're 5 Years Old"
---

Think of your identity as a name tag and your Homeserver as a locker. Apps use the name tag to find your locker. You can choose another locker without changing your name tag.

Moving lockers still means moving your belongings: changing the address does not recover missing data. [Credible Exit](/explore/concepts/credible-exit/) explains why keeping a copy matters.

For the actual components behind this analogy, read the [protocol introduction](/explore/pubky-protocol/introduction/).
