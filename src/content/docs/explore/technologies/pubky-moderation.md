title: "Pubky Moderation"
---

Synonym moderates its hosted [Homeserver](/explore/pubky-protocol/homeserver/) and [Nexus](/explore/pubky-apps/indexing-and-aggregation/pubky-nexus/) to combat harmful content. If you run your own homeserver, you are responsible for its moderation.

For support with homeserver moderation, reach out to the Synonym team on [pubky.app](https://pubky.app/profile/e3hepg9z5wan8yk969mbod839oj4jaisgaq5ifkn19hbscafuzeo) or [Telegram](https://t.me/synonym_to).
